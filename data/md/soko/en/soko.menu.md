## General assortment: sushi, soups and salads

category: assortment
language: en
tags: [menu, availability, sushi, soups, salads]

Categories available for order: Rolls, Chains, Bowls, Maki, Gunkan, Nigiri, Baked rolls, Tempura Rolls (Hot). 
Also available are sauces (soy, wasabi, ginger), soups (miso soup) and traditional Chuka salad made from pickled seaweed.

## What are Rolls
group_id: desc_rolls_base
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [rolls, classic, nori]
---
Rolls are the general name for dishes where rice and filling are rolled into a roll using a sheet of nori (seaweed). This roll is then cut into 6 or 8 equal pieces.

## Sets (Sets)
group_id: desc_sets_bundles
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [sets, packages, benefits, company]
---
Networks are ready-made sets consisting of different types of rolls and sushi. A profitable option for a large company or if you want to try several flavors at once.

## Bowls and Poke
group_id: desc_bowls_poke
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [bowl, poke, deconstructed sushi, fish]
---
Bouli (Poke) is a dish in a deep bowl. This is “deconstructed sushi”: pieces of raw fish, vegetables, edamame beans, chuka are placed on a bed of rice and topped with a special sauce.

## Poppies
group_id: desc_maki_simple
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [maki, simple rolls, classic]
---
Maki - classic, simple rolls. They are usually thin, wrapped with seaweed on the outside, and only have one type of filling inside (for example, salmon only, cucumber only, or tuna).

## Gunkans (Ships)
group_id: desc_gunkan_ships
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [gunkans, boats, caviar, tartare]
---
Gunkans means “ships”. It is an oval base of rice, wrapped in a tall strip of nora, and topped with filling (caviar, finely chopped fish or spicy tartare).

## Nigiri
group_id: desc_nigiri_classic
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [nigiri, traditional sushi, rice, fish]
---
Nigiri is the most traditional type of sushi. This is an oblong lump of rice compressed with the palm, on which a piece of fresh fish, shrimp or eel is simply placed on top.

## Baked rolls
group_id: desc_baked_rolls
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [baked rolls, warm rolls, spicy sauce]
---
Baked rolls are cold rolls, on which sauce (often curd or spicy) is applied on top and baked in the oven until an appetizing crust appears. Served warm.

## Tempura Rolls (Hot)
group_id: desc_tempura_hot_rolls
category: description_of-dishes
language: ru
tags: [tempura, hot rolls, deep-fried, batter]
---
Tempura Rolls (Hot) - rolls that are completely dipped in batter (tempura) and deep-fried until golden crisp. They have a rich taste and are served hot.
